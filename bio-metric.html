Our Unique Selling Points

Multi-Biometric Access

Multi-Biometric Access secures gym entry via face recognition, fingerprints,& RFID, blocks inactive members, prevents proxy access, tracks attendance, and provides centralized, automated control across all the branches.

Automatic Access Control

Auto prevent access efficiently blocks inactive plans, ensures only verified active members enter, eliminates unauthorized access, reduces front-desk work, prevents revenue loss, and provides secure, automated control.

Instant Membership Reactivation

Gym owners can quickly resume expired, frozen, or paused memberships with instant reactivation, which automatically updates access, billing, and attendance for a smooth member experience and helps increase revenue.

Attendance

By offering real-time visibility into member & staff check-ins, connecting attendance with memberships, PT sessions, classes, preventing revenue leakage, and producing reports, Easy Gym Software's attendance feature increases efficiency, discipline, and retention.

Smart QR Check-Ins

Track attendance across gyms, studios, and classes with QR-based check-ins for secure, contactless tracking. Data-driven insights and membership verification help monitor trends, boost retention, and provide clients and staff with a modern experience.

RFID

RFID-powered attendance makes gym and studio management effortless. Secure, contactless check-ins via cards, bands, keychains save time, reduce manual work, track engagement, improve retention, optimize eco-friendly operations for gyms, studios, & academies.

Fingerprint & Facial Recognition

Fingerprint & facial biometrics enable secure, touchless attendance for gyms and studios. With a fully integrated system that complies with data privacy regulations, streamline operations, prevent fraud, guarantee hygiene, simplify client management, and save costs.

Turnstile Integration

Using RFID or biometrics, turnstile integration provides smooth, touchless entry. Automate access, attendance, and billing while owners gain real-time insights. Secure, efficient, and customizable, it modernizes data management for gyms, studios, and fitness centers.
