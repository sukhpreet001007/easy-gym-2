Reporting

Our Unique Selling Points

Insightful Custom Reports

Gyms can confidently identify trends, close gaps, improve operations, and increase overall profitability with the help of custom reports, which offer real-time, actionable insight on revenue, memberships, trainers, attendance, & sales.

Export to Excel Instantly

Export to Excel Instantly lets gym owners download memberships, attendance, payments, and more, saving time, simplifying analysis, ensuring accurate records, and enabling smarter, faster data-driven business decisions.

Daily Reports via Email

Daily Reports via Email provide gym owners with automated summaries of admissions, renewals, attendance, revenue, and follow-ups, ensuring effortless tracking, timely insights, and smarter business decisions.

Sales & Retention

Sales & Renewal feature fully automates memberships, upgrades, and renewals. Transform renewals into steady, stress-free income by tracking inquiries, conversions, payments in real time, sending automated reminders, gaining insights, and increasing retention.

Clients

Make data-driven decisions by tracking client revenue, promotions, and best-selling services. Centralize member details with automated renewals, interactions, while effortlessly managing attendance, billing, & access for secure, efficient gym, club, or studio operations.

Payments

Take full control of your gym’s revenue with Easy Gym Software’s Payment & Collection system. Automate payments, track transactions, send instant invoices, and get renewal reminders via email, text, and WhatsApp, ensuring secure, efficient, and transparent cash flow.

Staff

The Staff feature lets gym owners easily manage their team from a single dashboard. Track attendance, shifts, leaves, and performance; assign tasks; link activity to revenue; and gain transparency, boosting accountability with enhanced productivity and smooth gym operations.

Inventory

Easily track stock, sales, and expiry with automatic low-stock alerts. Simplify product management, purchases, and reordering on Easy Gym Software’s secure cloud interface. Get instant reports on stock, performance, profits, reducing waste & ensuring smooth operations.
