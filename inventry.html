Our Unique Selling Points

Automated WhatsApp

Automated WhatsApp boosts member retention and revenue with automatic reminders, expiry alerts, and timely follow-ups. No app downloads, zero manual effort, and 24/7 operation reliably drive growth and save time.

Biometric Authentication

Biometric authentication in software controls entry, attendance, and revenue. It blocks expired memberships, prevents proxy entries, and syncs with billing to reduce effort and ensure a premium, disciplined environment.

Multi-Location User Access

Multi-location access in software lets owners manage all branches from a single dashboard. Monitor performance, sales, and attendance with role-based access and centralized billing for scalable growth and expansion.

QR Attendance

Track and record attendance across your gym, pilates, MMA, yoga, and fitness studio from one dynamic platform. QR-based check-in offers secure, synchronized tracking, saves staff time, and provides fast, contactless, and authentic affairs using smartphones. Integrated membership plans automatically verify active plans and detect expired ones to ensure authorized access. Data-driven insights enable you to monitor trends, enhance retention by implementing smart business tactics, and deliver a modernized experience for both your clients and team.

Smart Access Control

Our gym management software includes a powerful Prevent Access feature that automatically blocks entry for members with expired memberships. Integrated with biometric smart check-in, ensures only active members can access the facilities. This system is quite simple and reliable, as it saves staff time, enhances security, revenue, and operations via an easy-to-use, dynamic platform.

RFID

Flawless gym operations with RFID-powered attendance tracking. Entry of Members became easier using cards, bands, or keychains, diminishing manual check-ins and waiting times. Recommended by dance, fitness, studios, yoga centers, sports academies, or gyms, our software ensures a secure, effortless, and contactless experience. An Eco-friendly, paperless system with enhanced security ensures authorized access only, updated attendance tracking monitors engagement.

Finger Scan & face

Fingerprint biometric systems provide a secure app-free solution for attendance tracking, used by gyms, fitness, pilates studios, and sports centers. Members can enter with a fingerprint, eliminating the cards or PINs. Enhanced security and efficiency by preventing fraud with automatic attendance tracking and reducing administrative efforts.

Facial Scan

Level up the biometrics of your gym with ZKTECO, ESSL face recognition devices suitable for fast, secure access. Enhancing security by preventing unauthorized touchless check-ins reduces unwanted access to premises. Promoting hygiene via hands-free check-in is ideal for post-COVID safety. Automatic attendance tracking enhances efficiency with flawless integration and simplifies the setup of gym management software.
