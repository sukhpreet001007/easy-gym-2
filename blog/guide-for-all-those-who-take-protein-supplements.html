Guide For All Those Who Take Protein Supplements

Choosing the right protein supplement is crucial for achieving fitness goals

Taking protein for gaining weight and building physique! How would you consider this statement? Is this okay, or not?

Some of you would say, no! It's not a good option. Contrarily, there might also be some people, mainly gym freaks, who might consider it nothing wrong. Well, here both the answers are right.

Protein, whether grabbed through natural measures such as eggs, chicken, fish, milk, almonds, peas, and lentils, or even artificial ways such as protein powders and supplements is okay for your body, if you take them in a satisfactory amount.

However, the majority of the population believes that taking protein powders and supplements is not good for health. But that's not true in all cases. People experience side effects only when they take these supplements either in excessive quantities or in an unprescribed way.

If you are taking these protein supplements in the right dosage, at the right time, and in a manner suggested by a physician, and still experiencing any kind of problem. Then it's not you but the brand of protein you have chosen.

Yes! Yes! We got you. Now, you might ask "Which brand's protein is best?"

Then here we are, and our blog. Reading today's blog will let you know the top 7 Protein supplement brands in India.

Importance of Protein

It is a vital macronutrient that supports muscle growth, repair, and overall functions of the body. It is also necessary as it helps in:

Building/Repairing tissues

It is important for your muscle recovery and growth after workouts and physical activities.

Weight management

Helps to keep you at an ideal weight. You will neither be obese, nor thin, maintaining healthy body composition.

Boosting metabolism

Through this, calories are burnt and metabolic rate is increased, aiding in weight loss and energy production.

How to Choose the Right Protein for Your Body

Well, the aforementioned proteins, and even multiple other brands can be helpful for you or may benefit your body in several ways possible. However, you should keep in mind certain things while going for a particular brand. These areas to be considered are as follows:

The content of protein:

Firstly, make sure that the product you are going for contains a sufficient amount of protein per serving.

Its ingredients:

Now see how many additives and artificial ingredients it contains. Opt for products with minimal artificial additives.

How much is the digestibility:

Choose a product that is easy on your stomach. Make sure it is easy to digest and causes no harm to your body.

The reputation of the brand:

It is highly desirable to go for reputable brands as they are trusted by thousands of people already. We have already mentioned these brands to you.

How it is priced:

Last but still important, also consider the cost per serving. Make sure you are choosing the one that fits both your budget and body.

Additional Tips For Protein Consumption

Make sure you consume protein within 30 minutes post-workout for optimal muscle recovery.

Don't be fully dependent on these supplements. Make sure to add some natural sources of protein in your diet too. For this, you may take chicken, fish, eggs, legumes, etc.

Always drink plenty of water so that it can aid in protein digestion and muscle recovery.

Always consult with a nutritionist, or healthcare provider before starting any supplement, especially if you have dietary restrictions or health conditions.
